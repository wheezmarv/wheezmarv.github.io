CASE STUDY 01 · N8N · AI LEAD OPERATIONS

AI Lead Qualification & Follow-up System

Problem: Enquiries can arrive at different times and require manual review, sorting, and follow-up. Slow responses can mean missed opportunities.

Solution approach: An n8n workflow designed to capture enquiries, use AI to extract key details such as service needs, budget, and urgency, support lead scoring, save structured lead data, and trigger tailored follow-up steps.

Tools: n8n, OpenAI/AI processing, and connected CRM or database services depending on the configured workflow.

Intended impact: Faster lead triage, more consistent follow-up, better-organised enquiry records, and less repetitive admin. These are target outcomes, not measured performance claims.
